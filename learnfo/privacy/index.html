Privacy Policy — One Thought Still

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · 繁體中文

One Thought Still is a quiet practice app: one stick of incense, a pond, and the days you have counted. It asks you to sign in so your days, beads, colors, and purchases follow you to another phone. Your own audio files never leave your device.

1. What We Collect

DataWhat happensCollected by us
Email addressProvided by you or by Sign in with Apple (which may be a private relay address)Yes, linked to you
Account IDCreated when you sign in, so your data can be tied to your accountYes, linked to you
Practice recordsSession length, planned and practiced seconds, whether you ended early, sound mode, daily credit, local date, and time zoneYes, linked to you
PreferencesPreferred length, lotus color, sound choice, tap hint, interface languageYes, linked to you
Display nameThe name your accepted companions see; you choose and can change itYes, linked to you

Your audio recordingsStored only inside the App on your deviceNo
Contacts, photos, location, advertising ID, analyticsThe App does not ask for or collect any of theseNo

We use this data only to run the App: to sign you in, to count your days and beads, to show your companions that you are practicing, and to restore what you bought. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or tracking, and it is never sold.

2. What Stays on Your Device

  • Copies of audio files you add to the audio library — the original file is never modified;
  • Local settings, such as your last chosen sound and the tap hint;
  • The scene itself. The lotus, water, and incense are drawn on your device and are not uploaded.

Deleting an audio item in the App removes only the App's copy. Deleting the App removes these local files.

3. Accounts

An account is required. You sign in with Apple or with an email and password. Account data is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database as a data processor. We never receive your App Store password or payment details.

4. Companions (同修)

Companions are invite-only and one-to-one. An accepted companion can see your display name, whether you are online, and whether you are practicing at that moment. There is no public profile, no discovery, and no messaging. Presence is live only while the App is open. You can remove a companion at any time, which ends that visibility.

5. Purchases

One Thought Still Plus (monthly, yearly, or one-time) and 隨喜 offering packs are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We record which plan or balance you own so it can be restored on another phone, and we verify purchases with Apple's server API. Offerings are consumable gifts between companions; they do not unlock content and do not change anyone's progress.

6. Analytics, Advertising, and Third Parties

The App contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking SDKs, and it does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. The only third-party service that handles your account data is Supabase, which hosts our database and realtime service. No data is shared with or sold to anyone else.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Account data is kept until you delete your account. In the App, open Settings and choose 刪除帳號 (Delete account). This permanently removes your account and its server-side data: practice records, preferences, companions, offerings, and purchase records. You can also email us to request deletion. Files stored on your phone, including copies of your own audio, stay until you remove them in Settings or delete the App.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or the App: alfa_lab@sina.com

隱私權政策 — 一念止

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 30 日

一念止是一個安靜的修習 App：一炷香、一池水，和你數過的日子。登入是為了讓你的天數、佛珠、顏色與購買跟著帳號走到另一支手機；你自己的錄音不會離開這台裝置。

2. 只留在裝置上的東西

  • 你加入音訊庫的音檔副本——原始檔案不會被修改；
  • 本機設定，例如上次選的音與提示語開關；
  • 畫面本身。蓮花、水與香都在你的裝置上繪製，不會上傳。

在 App 裡刪除音訊只會刪掉 App 內的副本；刪除 App 會一併移除這些本機檔案。

3. 帳號

需要登入。你可以用「透過 Apple 登入」或電子郵件加密碼登入。帳號資料存放在 Supabase，它作為資料處理者托管我們的資料庫。我們不會收到你的 App Store 密碼或付款資料。

4. 同修

同修只能透過邀請、一對一建立。對方可以看到你的顯示名稱、是否在線，以及當下是否正在練習。沒有公開頁面、沒有搜尋、沒有私訊；在線狀態只在 App 開啟時有效。你隨時可以移除同修，移除後對方就看不到這些狀態。

5. 購買

一念止 Plus（按月、按年或買斷）與隨喜包都由 Apple 透過 App Store 處理。我們只記錄你擁有的方案或餘額，以便在另一支手機恢復，並透過 Apple 的伺服器 API 驗證交易。隨喜是消耗型的同修贈禮，不解鎖內容，也不改變任何人的進度。

6. 分析、廣告與第三方

App 沒有分析工具、沒有廣告、沒有追蹤 SDK，也不會要求 App 追蹤透明度權限。唯一處理帳號資料的第三方是 Supabase（資料庫與即時連線）。資料不會分享或販售給其他人。

7. 資料保留與刪除

帳號資料保留到你刪除帳號為止。在 App 的「設定」中選擇「刪除帳號」，會永久移除帳號與伺服器上的資料：練習紀錄、偏好、同修、隨喜與購買紀錄。你也可以寫信請我們刪除。留在手機上的檔案（包括你自己的音檔副本）會保留到你自行移除或刪除 App。

8. 兒童隱私

本 App 並非提供給 13 歲以下兒童，也不會刻意收集其資料。若你發現兒童建立了帳號，請與我們聯絡，我們會刪除該帳號。

9. 政策變更

若政策有變更，會更新上方的生效日期；重大變更也會寫在版本更新說明中。

10. 聯絡

對本政策或 App 有疑問：alfa_lab@sina.com
