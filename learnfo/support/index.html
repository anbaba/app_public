One stick of incense, one pond · 繁體中文

One Thought Still is a quiet place to sit. There are no levels, no streaks to protect, and no notifications asking you to come back. Most questions come down to one idea: a session is one stick of incense.

Common questions

How do I start a session?

On the home screen, tap the piece of wood floating on the water. The incense lights and begins to burn. Tapping the incense or the empty water does nothing by design, so the start is deliberate.

How long is a session?

Fifteen, thirty, forty-five, or sixty minutes. Choose the length in the session row before you start. The incense is the timer; there is no countdown to watch.

Can I stop early?

Yes. While practicing, tap anywhere on the screen. A session of at least one minute is saved; anything shorter is not recorded.

Why didn't my practice count?

Two common reasons: the session was shorter than one minute, or you had already practiced earlier that day. One day counts at most one point, however many sessions you sit.

Why hasn't the lotus opened?

The lotus grows with days, not minutes. One point per day, and eighty-one points complete a cycle: the lotus seeds, you receive a bead, and the pond starts again.

Can I use my own recording?

Yes. Add a file in the session settings or in Settings → Audio library. The App keeps its own copy; your original file is never modified or deleted. All sounds are free, and your recording plays as a quiet background while the incense burns.

What are companions (同修)?

An invite-only, one-to-one way to sit alongside someone. An accepted companion can see your name and whether you are practicing right now; there is no messaging and no public profile. You can send a small offering (隨喜), which never changes anyone's progress, and you can remove a companion at any time.

What does One Thought Still Plus unlock?

Only the lotus colors: white, jade, pink, red, gold, and the two bicolors, Pink Tip and Brocade. The practice itself, every sound, the timer, and your statistics are free.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Plus screen and choose Restore purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account that bought it.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. The first launch asks once; after that the App remembers your choice.

Does it work offline?

You can sit without a connection, but a session is recorded on our server, so it needs one to be counted. If saving fails, the App tells you and that session is not added to your days.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → 刪除帳號 (Delete account). This permanently removes your account, practice records, preferences, companions, offerings, and purchase records from our database. Files on your phone, including copies of your own audio, stay until you remove them or delete the App.

支援 — 一念止

一炷香，一池水

一念止是一個安靜坐下來的地方。沒有等級、沒有要守住的連續紀錄，也不會發通知催你回來。大部分問題都繞著同一件事：一次練習，就是一炷香。

常見問題

怎麼開始練習？

在首頁點一下漂在水面的浮木，香就會點起來。點香本身或空白水面不會有反應，這是刻意的——開始要是一個明確的動作。

一次多長？

十五、三十、四十五或六十分鐘。開始前在練習設定列選好長度。香就是計時器，不需要盯著倒數。

可以提早結束嗎？

可以。練習中點一下畫面任何位置即可結束。滿一分鐘的練習會保存，不足一分鐘不記錄。

為什麼這次沒有算進去？

常見兩種情況：練習不足一分鐘，或你當天已經練習過。一天最多只計一點，不論你坐了幾次。

蓮花怎麼還沒開？

蓮花按「天」長，不按分鐘。每天一點，八十一點走完一輪：蓮花結子、你得到一顆佛珠，池子重新開始。

可以用自己的錄音嗎？

可以。在練習設定或「設定 → 音訊庫」加入檔案。App 會保留一份副本，你的原始檔案不會被修改或刪除。所有聲音都免費，你的錄音會當作背景音，陪著香慢慢燒。

同修是什麼？

一種只能透過邀請、一對一的同行方式。對方可以看到你的名字、以及你當下是否正在練習；沒有私訊，也沒有公開頁面。你可以送出一點隨喜（不影響任何人的進度），也隨時可以移除同修。

一念止 Plus 解鎖什麼？

只解鎖蓮花顏色：白、青、粉、紅、金，以及複色的「小舞妃」與「灑錦」。練習本身、所有聲音、計時與統計都是免費的。

怎麼恢復購買？

打開 Plus 頁面，選「恢復購買」。請確認裝置登入的是當初購買的 Apple 帳號。

怎麼取消訂閱？

訂閱由 Apple 管理：iOS「設定」→ 你的 Apple 帳號 →「訂閱項目」。刪除 App 不會取消訂閱。

怎麼改語言？

「設定 → 語言」。第一次啟動會問一次，之後會記住你的選擇。

離線可以用嗎？

沒有連線也能坐，但練習要寫回伺服器才會計入。儲存失敗時 App 會提示，這次練習不會加進你的天數。

怎麼刪除帳號？

「設定 → 刪除帳號」。這會永久移除帳號、練習紀錄、偏好、同修、隨喜與購買紀錄。手機上的檔案（包括你自己的音檔副本）會保留到你自行移除或刪除 App。
