Privacy Policy — Slow Spell

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Slow Spell is a quiet timer: a jellyfish, a countdown, and a bell. It opens without an account. A guest session is created on first launch, and your practice, preferences, and color are saved to it. Signing in with Apple or email is optional; it is needed only for Friends mode and for carrying your data to another phone.

The App contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking SDKs. It does not use the Advertising Identifier and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.

1. What We Collect

DataWhat it is and when it is collected
Email addressProvided by you or by Sign in with Apple (which may be a private relay address) if you choose to sign in.
User IDAn identifier for your guest session or signed-in account, used to match your data to you.
Display nameThe name shown to your friends. It defaults to part of your email or Apple name and can be changed in the App.

We use this data only to run the App: to keep your guest session or sign you in, count your sessions, show Friends, keep your energy balance, and restore purchases. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold.

2. What Stays on Your Device

  • Audio files you add to the App — the original file is never modified or uploaded;
  • Local settings, such as your chosen length, color, sound, and tap hint;
  • A local copy of your preferences and recent session state, so the App still works without a connection;
  • The scene itself. The jellyfish and water are drawn on your device and are not uploaded.

Deleting an audio item in the App removes only the App's copy. Deleting the App removes these local files.

3. Accounts and Guest Sessions

An account is optional. On first launch the App creates a guest session. Practice records, preferences, and colors are stored under it. You can sign in with Apple or with an email and password at any time. When you sign in from a guest session, that guest data stays with the account, and the guest session is linked to your Apple or email identity. Account and guest data are stored with Supabase, which hosts our database as a data processor. We never receive your Apple Account password or your payment details.

4. Friends

Friends mode is invite-only and private. There is no user search, public profile, feed, comments, likes, or chat, and you cannot post or share text, photos, or other content. A friend can see your display name, your jellyfish color, whether you are currently practicing, and any glow you send. You can have up to 20 friends and remove any friend at any time. Friends mode requires a signed-in account; guest sessions cannot use it.

5. Purchases and Energy Bait

Slow Spell Plus (monthly, yearly, or lifetime) is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Energy bait is an optional consumable sold in packs and used one at a time to send a glow to a friend. We record your balance and the transactions needed to keep it accurate. Energy bait has no cash value, cannot be transferred, and is not refundable through the App; refunds are handled by Apple. It is never required to practice, change colors, or use Plus features.

6. Third Parties

Supabase hosts our database and authentication. Apple processes App Store purchases and Sign in with Apple. These providers handle data on our behalf or as independent payment and sign-in providers. No data is shared with or sold to anyone else, and no advertising or analytics providers are used.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is kept while your guest session or account exists. In Settings you can choose Delete account (for a signed-in account) or Delete guest data (for a guest session). This immediately deletes the account and its server-side data — practice records, preferences, friends, energy, and purchase records — and signs you out. It cannot be undone. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to revoke the stored credential. Deleting the App without deleting the guest session leaves that session on the server; you can remove it from Settings before deleting the App, or contact us.

Subscriptions are managed by Apple and are not cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel them in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created a guest session or account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy.
