Common Questions

Do I need an account?

No. The App starts a guest session on first launch, and your practice, preferences, and color are saved to it. Signing in with Apple or email is optional. A signed-in account is needed for Friends mode and for carrying your data to another phone.

How do I start or end a session?

Tap the jellyfish to begin. Tap it again to end early. The timer keeps running in the background, so you can lock the phone or leave the room. A bell marks the end. No notifications are used.

How do I choose a length or a recording?

Tap the “N min · Sound” line under the jellyfish. The session sheet offers the built-in forest recordings (12, 15, 20, and 30 minutes) and Silent for the self timer.

How do Friends work?

Switch from Just me to Friends at the top left of the home screen. Friends are added only through an invite link; there is no search, public profile, feed, chat, likes, or comments. You can have up to 20 friends and remove any friend at any time. Friends mode requires a signed-in account.

What is energy bait?

Energy bait is an optional consumable used to send a small glow to a friend. One bait is used each time you send a glow. Unused bait stays in your account, has no cash value, and cannot be transferred or refunded through the App. It never changes practice progress and is not required for any feature.

How do I manage or cancel Slow Spell Plus?

Plus is purchased through your Apple Account. To manage or cancel, open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Deleting the App or deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings → See plans, then choose Restore purchases.

How do I delete my account or guest data?

Open Settings and choose Delete account (signed-in) or Delete guest data (guest session). This permanently deletes the account and all associated data — practice records, preferences, friends, energy, and purchase records — and signs you out. It cannot be undone. Subscriptions are not cancelled by deleting your account.

Do my audio files leave my phone?

No. Files you add to the App are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.

Is Slow Spell a medical device?

No. Slow Spell is a quiet timer and does not provide medical advice or treatment.
