Privacy Policy — Foible Camera

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Foible Camera ("the App") is a film camera that runs entirely on your device. This policy explains what happens to your information. In short: the App collects nothing, and your photos never leave your device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. The App has no account system, no sign-in, no server, no analytics, and no advertising.

DataWhat happensCollected by us
Photos and development notesStored only on your device and in the App's widget containerNo
App settingsStored only on your deviceNo
Purchase historyProcessed by Apple; the App stores entitlement state locallyNo
Usage, analytics, identifiersThe App does not generate or send anyNo

2. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Frames you shoot, and the development notes generated for each roll;
  • App settings, such as sound and camera preferences;
  • Purchase state, so the App knows which rolls and unlocks you own.

This information stays in the App's local storage and the shared container used by the home screen widget. It is never uploaded anywhere.

3. Camera and Photo Library

Camera access is used only to capture frames while you shoot. The App never reads your photo library. The add-only photo library permission is requested only if you choose to save a contact sheet or development video to Photos.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases (the one-time unlock and film packs) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment details, and the App verifies purchases locally on the device. Consumable film packs are not restored after a reinstall; the one-time unlock can be restored from the Hangar screen.

5. Analytics, Advertising, and Third Parties

The App contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs, and no third-party services. No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect data, there is nothing for us to retain. Deleting the App removes all frames, notes, and settings from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
