Privacy Policy — Foible Camera

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Foible Camera ("the App") is a film camera. Your photos never leave your device. The App asks you to sign in before you shoot, so the unlock and your film counts always have an owner. Signing in stores a small amount of account and purchase data with our database host, so your camera follows you to another phone.

1. What We Collect

DataWhat happensCollected by us
Photos and development notesStored only on your device and in the App's widget containerNo
App settingsStored only on your deviceNo
Email address and account IDStored in our database when you sign in, so you can sign in on your devicesYes, linked to you
Purchase historyApple processes the payment; we store which unlock and film packs you own, and your remaining film rolls, so they can be restoredYes, linked to you
Usage, analytics, advertising identifiersThe App does not collect or send anyNo

We use this data only to run the App: to sign you in, to remember what you own, and to restore it on another device. It is not used for tracking, advertising, or profiling, and it is never sold.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Frames you shoot, and the development notes generated for each roll;
  • App settings, such as sound and camera preferences;
  • A local copy of your purchase state and film counts, so the App works offline.

Frames and notes stay in the App's local storage and the shared container used by the home screen widget. They are never uploaded anywhere.

3. Accounts

An account is required, and you sign in at launch with Apple or an email and password. Account data (email address, account ID, purchases, and film balances) is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database as a data processor. Photos, rolls, and development notes are never part of your account.

4. Camera and Photo Library

Camera access is used only to capture frames while you shoot. The App never reads your photo library. The add-only photo library permission is requested only if you choose to save a contact sheet or development video to Photos.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases (the one-time unlock and film packs) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment details. The App verifies purchases on the device and records the resulting unlock and film balances in your account so they can be restored on another phone.

6. Analytics, Advertising, and Third Parties

The App contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking SDKs. The only third-party service that handles your account data is Supabase, which hosts our database. No data is shared with or sold to anyone else.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Account data is kept until you delete your account. In the App, open Account and choose Delete account; this removes your account, purchase record, and film balances from our database. You can also email us to request deletion. Deleting the App removes all frames, notes, and settings from your device; your unlock and film balances remain with your account and can be restored after reinstalling.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
