Support — Foible Camera

Film photography, no preview

Foible Camera is a film camera, not a photo app. There is no preview, and your photos never leave your device. You sign in before you shoot, so the unlock and your film counts always have an owner. Most questions come down to one idea: a roll stays sealed until the lab is done with it.

Common questions

Why can't I see my photos?

There is no preview. A roll stays sealed until it is developed at the lab, 24 hours after you send it.

Where are my photos stored?

On this device only. They are never uploaded, and deleting the App deletes them.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You sign in with Apple or an email and password at launch. The account holds the unlock and your film counts so they can be restored on another phone; photos, rolls, prints and notes stay on the device.

How do I see a roll?

Open the Studio and the rolls drawer, send the roll to the lab, wait for the timer, then open the darkroom. The completed roll appears as a contact sheet with development notes on the back.

Why did a frame come out scratched, leaked, or off?

Those come from the film chemistry and this particular camera body: exposure, grain, light leaks, a shutter that runs a little slow. They are not filters, and they are not a bug.

How do I unlock the camera or buy film?

The first roll is free. After that, the Hangar sells the one-time unlock, and extra film packs are sold when you load a roll or in the rolls drawer. Restore purchases in the Hangar brings the unlock and your film balances back on any phone signed in to the same account.

Does the App need an internet connection?

Signing in needs a connection, and so do purchases and restores. Once you are signed in, shooting, developing and exporting work offline.

Can I move my rolls to a new phone?

Your frames stay on the phone that shot them; they are never uploaded. Your unlock and film balances follow your account, so sign in on the new phone to restore those. To keep the photos themselves, export the contact sheet or the development video before switching devices.

How do I delete my account?

Open Account and choose Delete account. This removes your account, purchase record, and film balances from our database. Frames and notes on this phone are untouched; delete the App to remove those.
