Support — Foible Camera

Film photography, no preview

Foible Camera is a film camera, not a photo app. There is no preview, no account, and nothing leaves your device. Most questions come down to one idea: a roll stays sealed until the lab is done with it.

Common questions

Why can't I see my photos?

There is no preview. A roll stays sealed until it is developed at the lab, a few hours after you send it.

Where are my photos stored?

On this device only. They are never uploaded, and deleting the App deletes them.

How do I see a roll?

Open the roll shelf, send the roll to the lab, wait for the timer, then open the darkroom. The completed roll appears as a contact sheet with development notes on the back.

Why did a frame come out scratched, leaked, or off?

Those come from the film chemistry and this particular camera body: exposure, grain, light leaks, a shutter that runs a little slow. They are not filters, and they are not a bug.

How do I unlock the camera or buy film?

The Hangar sells the one-time unlock and extra film packs. Restore Purchases in the Hangar restores the unlock. Consumable film packs are used up when shot and are not restored after a reinstall.

Does the App need an internet connection?

No. Everything works offline, except StoreKit, which needs a connection the first time you buy or restore.

Can I move my rolls to a new phone?

No. Rolls and notes live in the App's local storage, and there is no account or cloud sync. Export the contact sheet or the development video before switching devices if you want to keep a copy.
