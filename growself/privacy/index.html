Privacy Policy — GrowSelf

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data GrowSelf ("the App") collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep data collection minimal, and we never sell your data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To keep your goals, check-ins and notes on this device, and to sync them across your devices when you sign in;
  • To show you your own history — goals, tasks, check-ins, and notes — in the App;
  • To run the optional Friends feature you choose to use;
  • To verify and deliver subscription entitlements (GrowSelf Plus).

3. Accounts and Sign-In

You can use the App without creating an account. On first launch the App creates a guest account for this device through our authentication provider (Supabase Auth); your goals, check-ins and notes are saved under that guest account, and no email address or name is required. If you choose Sign in with Apple (Me → Sign in with Apple), we receive your Apple Account identifier and, if you allow it, your email address, and your existing guest data is linked to that account so it can follow you to another device. Existing email accounts, such as the App Review account, sign in with an email address and password; the password is stored only as a secure one-way hash by Supabase Auth, and we never see or store it in readable form.

4. Friends

Friends is optional and requires a signed-in account. If you use it, we store your display name, a generated friend code, the invitations you send or accept, the friend connections you accept, and any reports or blocks you create. By default friends cannot see your goals, check-ins or notes. If you turn on mood sharing, friends you have accepted can see up to three of your moods from the past week; journal text is never shared. There is no public feed, comments, or chat.

5. Purchases & Subscriptions

GrowSelf Plus subscriptions (monthly, yearly) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your card details. Subscription charges, auto-renewal, and cancellation are managed in your Apple ID settings.

6. Storage

Your data is stored on servers provided by Supabase (AWS, US East). We limit access to your data to the technical operations required to run the service.

7. No Advertising, No Tracking

The App contains no advertisements, uses no analytics SDKs, and does not share your data with third parties for any purpose other than providing the App.

8. Children

The App is rated 4+ and is not directed at collecting personal information from children beyond what is needed to provide the App.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your data only as long as needed to provide the App. You can delete your account and all associated data in the App: open Me and choose Delete Account. You may also request deletion by contacting the email below. Deleting the App removes its data from your device; if you did not sign in, the guest account and its records remain on our servers until deleted in the App or on request.

10. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS encryption, and access to stored data is restricted to the technical operations required to run the service.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and make the updated policy available in the App and on the App Store page.
