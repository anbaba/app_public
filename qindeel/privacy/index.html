Privacy Policy — Qindeel: Quran Guide

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data Qindeel: Quran Guide ("Qindeel", "the App") collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep data collection minimal, and we never sell your data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To answer your questions with Quranic verses, translations, and commentary;
  • To maintain your personal features (favorites, notes, reading history, points);
  • To verify and deliver subscription entitlements.

3. Religious Information

Your questions and reading activity may reveal your religious background. This information is used solely to return Quranic results and to power your personal history. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to track you across apps or websites.

4. Sign in with Apple

You can sign in with Sign in with Apple. We receive only the information you allow Apple to share (your name or a relay email address). We do not collect or store passwords.

5. Purchases & Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions (monthly, yearly) and the one-time Lifetime unlock are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your card details. Subscription charges, auto-renewal, and cancellation are managed in your Apple ID settings.

6. Children

The App is rated 12+. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent.

7. No Advertising, No Tracking

The App contains no advertisements, does not use device identifiers for advertising, and does not share your data with third parties for any purpose other than providing the App.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your data only as long as needed to provide the App. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting the email below.

9. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS) for all data in transit and limit access to your data to the technical operations required to run the service.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and make the updated policy available in the App and on the App Store page.
