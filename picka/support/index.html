Support — Picka

Dressed for the weather, from your own closet.

Picka looks at today's weather and the clothes you already own, then puts together an outfit you can actually wear. Most questions come down to one idea: the closet is the source of truth, and the weather decides what rises to the top.

Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. Picka opens in guest mode and weather planning, your closet, saved outfits, and wear history all work right away. Your guest data belongs to this installation of the app. To keep it when you reinstall or switch devices, open Me and choose Sign in with Apple. Buying credits or subscribing does require an account, so purchases can be restored later.

How do I add clothes?

Open Closet and tap Add an item. Pick a photo from your library or take one, then confirm the category, color, and warmth range. Auto-tagging can suggest those fields from the photo; you can always edit them by hand. Tap an item to edit it later, or drag to reorder within a category.

What is the difference between the two cutout options?

The free cutout runs on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework — the photo never leaves the device, and it works well on a plain background. The AI cutout sends the photo to our image provider, handles busier backgrounds better, costs 2 credits per item, and is part of Plus. Both produce a clean garment image for the outfit board; your closet thumbnail keeps the original photo.

Why does the app want my location?

To fetch today's weather from Apple WeatherKit. Picka asks for one approximate fix, only while you are using the app, and your coordinates are never sent to our servers. If you would rather not share location, tap Edit weather on Today and set the temperature, rain, wind, and UV by hand — the recommendations work exactly the same.

Outfits and scenes

How are outfits chosen?

Picka scores your clothes against the weather and the scene you pick, then fills each slot — top, bottom, one-piece, shoes, and outer layer. Hard rules come first: nothing that is too cold or too warm for the day, and nothing that breaks the scene. Softer preferences, like color harmony and not repeating yesterday's pieces, decide the order after that.

What does the scene picker do?

Scenes such as commute, office, weekend, or evening change the formality and layering the engine aims for. Pick a scene at the top of Today, or open the full list for more options.

What is a uniform day?

Turn it on when you need to wear the same thing as everyone else. Picka then limits suggestions to items you marked as uniform. If nothing is marked yet, Today will tell you instead of showing an empty outfit.

Can I change one piece without redoing everything?

Yes. Lock the slots you like and refresh the rest, or swipe a slot to see the next candidate. Recommend again rebuilds the whole look.

What does Log as worn do?

It records that you wore this combination today. Picka uses that history to rotate your clothes, so the same top does not come back three days running.

Try-on and the AI model

Do I have to upload a photo of myself?

No — Picka never asks for one. The person in a try-on preview is either one of ten bundled fashion models or a fictional AI model you generate from options like hair, body, skin tone, and style. Choose or create yours in Me → AI model. Only that model image and a garment photo are sent for generation.

How do I generate a try-on?

On Today, tap Try on this look. You can also try on a saved outfit from Closet. Generation takes a little while; the result appears when it is ready.

What does a try-on cost?

Each generated look uses 1 credit. One retry of the same look is free within 24 hours, and failed try-ons are refunded automatically. Free accounts get 1 credit each month plus 1 extra in the first week. Plus includes 30 credits each month, or 360 each year. You can also buy a 10-credit pack without subscribing.

Where did my try-on result go?

Generated results are deleted automatically 30 days after they are created. Save or screenshot anything you want to keep. Your closet, model image, and saved outfits are not affected.

Why does a try-on look off?

Generated previews are an approximation, not a photograph of you wearing the garment. Fit, drape, and exact color can differ. A clean garment photo on a plain background, and a model whose build is close to yours, give the best results. Use your free retry if the first attempt is clearly wrong.

Free and Plus

What can I do for free?

Weather planning, scenes, outfit scoring, wear history, saved outfits, the on-device cutout, and up to 20 closet items (8 tops, 6 bottoms, 2 outer layers, 2 dresses, 2 pairs of shoes). Free accounts also get 1 try-on credit a month.

What does Plus add?

An unlimited closet, 30 credits each month or 360 each year, and the AI cutout. Prices and any trial offer are shown in the app before you subscribe. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Me → Picka Plus and choose Restore purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy, and that you are signed in to Picka with Apple.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your device, open Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions, then choose Picka. Me → Manage subscription takes you to the same place. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

Do credits expire?

Credits stay valid while Picka's try-on service is available. If we ever discontinue it, we will give advance notice so you can use any remaining credits. Monthly Plus credits refresh each period; purchased packs do not.

Privacy and your account

Who can see my closet?

Only you. Picka has no feed, sharing, comments, or followers. Your items and photos are stored privately and are not visible to other users.

Are my photos used to train AI?

No. Garment photos are sent to an image provider only to produce the result you asked for, and we do not use your content to train models.

How do I delete my account?

Open Me → Delete account. It works for guest sessions too. This removes your account, closet, outfits, wear history, credits, entitlements, and stored images from our live systems, and revokes your sessions. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the Apple token. It does not cancel an App Store subscription. If you cannot sign in, email us from the address associated with your account and we will process the request within 30 days.

Does Picka work offline?

Your last loaded closet and weather stay visible, and the outfit board is built on-device. Syncing changes, fetching weather, and generating try-ons need a connection.

What is Demo mode?

A sample closet you can switch on in Me to explore the app without adding your own clothes. Turning it off returns you to your real closet.

A note on advice

Outfit suggestions are based on the weather values you or Apple WeatherKit provide. They are not safety advice — use your own judgement for extreme cold, heat, storms, or UV exposure.
