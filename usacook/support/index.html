Support — Dinner Handled

Dinner, decided.

Dinner Handled turns a grocery run into dinners you can actually cook. Photograph a receipt or shopping list, confirm what you bought, and get a few practical ideas for tonight. Most questions come down to one idea: one scan covers the week.

Getting started

How do I add groceries?

Tap Add groceries on the home screen. You can photograph a receipt or shopping list, scan a barcode, photograph a label, or type an item by hand. Nothing is saved until you review the list and confirm it.

How do I scan a receipt?

Choose the receipt option and take a photo of each page. You can include up to four pages in one scan, and the whole set counts as a single scan. On the confirmation screen, check the names, categories, and amounts before saving.

Why are some receipt lines missing?

Non-food lines such as bags, cleaning supplies, or pet food are skipped and listed separately as non-food. Blurry photos, folded paper, and unusual store formats can also hide a line. Add anything missing by hand; manual entry is always free.

What do Still have, Running low, Likely gone, and Use first mean?

These are rough estimates, not an exact inventory. Dinner Handled uses what you recently bought, how long ago you bought it, and what you have cooked to group items into four fuzzy levels. You can adjust an item's level in the kitchen list at any time. The app does not count units or track expiration dates.

Dinner ideas

Why do the ideas use only some of my items?

Dinner ideas are built around items marked Still have and Use first, so you are not asked to shop for a long list of missing ingredients. Items marked Running low may appear as optional additions and are labeled that way.

Do I have to generate ideas every time I open the app?

No. Nothing is generated until you tap Decide dinner or New ideas. A dinner you adopt stays on the home screen so you can open the method or mark it cooked later.

Can I plan a whole week of meals?

Dinner Handled is built for deciding tonight, not for a seven-day meal plan. Your weekly shopping list helps you restock; dinner ideas stay flexible so you can cook what sounds good that day.

What is the weekly shopping list?

It is one list per week, based on what your kitchen is running low on. You can edit it, add your own items, and check things off as you shop. Items you bought or edited stay when you rebuild the list. Last week's list is kept as a read-only record.

Keeping a list by hand is free. Building a list with AI is part of Premium.

Free and Premium

What can I do for free?

Manual entry, manual shopping lists, your kitchen, and your meal history are always free. Free accounts also get one scan per month and one set of dinner ideas per week. A free account can open the shopping list page and keep it by hand.

What does Premium include?

Premium currently includes up to 60 scans a month, up to 30 sets of dinner ideas a month, and up to 60 AI-built shopping lists a month. Prices and any trial offer are shown in the app before you subscribe. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Me and choose Restore purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your device, open Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions, then choose Dinner Handled. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

AI and privacy

Why does the app ask for AI permission?

Reading a photo, suggesting dinners, and building a shopping list use AI. The first time you use one of those features, the app explains what is sent and asks for permission. You can change your choice at any time in Me → AI & data. With AI off, those features ask again before sending anything; manual entry and manual lists keep working.

Are my photos stored?

Photos are sent to our backend and an AI model provider only to turn the image into a list of food items. They are not stored after the request completes. Avoid photographing faces, payment card numbers, or other sensitive information; crop or retake if needed.

How do I delete my account?

Open Me → Delete account. This removes your account and its data from our live systems and revokes your sessions. It does not cancel an App Store subscription. If you cannot sign in, email us from the address associated with your account and we will process the request within 30 days.

Does the app work offline?

Signing in, syncing your kitchen, recognition, and dinner ideas need a connection. Your last loaded kitchen and list may stay visible, but changes and new requests need to reach our servers.

Food safety

Dinner ideas and item levels are estimates, not food-safety or medical advice. Check labels for allergens and use-by dates, follow safe handling and cooking practices, and discard food that looks or smells unsafe.
